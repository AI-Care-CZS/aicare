<section class="home-pillars">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="section-head">
      <h2>What the consortium is tackling</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="row g-4">
      <div class="col-md-4">
        <article class="pillar">
          <h3>Cell-state plasticity</h3>
          <p>Glioblastoma cells switch phenotype and slip past standard therapy. Mapping that plasticity is the biological core of the project.</p>
        </article>
      </div>
      <div class="col-md-4">
        <article class="pillar">
          <h3>Models of response</h3>
          <p>Single-cell measurements are paired with machine learning, optimal control, and mechanistic models of how a tumour responds and recovers.</p>
        </article>
      </div>
      <div class="col-md-4">
        <article class="pillar">
          <h3>Treatment that adapts</h3>
          <p>The aim is therapy shaped to a tumour’s state, so resistance is less able to take hold — in glioblastoma and, in time, in other cancers.</p>
        </article>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
