The AI-Care consortium meets for a full day of group updates on Friday, 13 November 2026. Talks cover synthetic and single-cell data, glioblastoma organoids, surface proteomics, and mathematical models of cell-state dynamics.

<ul class="event-facts">
  <li><span>Date</span> Friday, 13 November 2026</li>
  <li><span>Time</span> 09:00–16:30</li>
  <li><span>Venue</span> Room 42-221, Department of Economics (Wirtschaftswissenschaften), RPTU Kaiserslautern</li>
</ul>
